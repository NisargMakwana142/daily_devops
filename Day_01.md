## What is DevOps?

DevOps is a **process and a culture**, not just a set of tools. It brings **Development** and **Operations** together.

More formally:  
DevOps is a software development methodology that combines and automates the work of software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to improve and shorten the systems development life cycle. It integrates people, processes, and technology to deliver continuous value to customers.

- IP addressing

- Routers vs. switches
